why this exists

i build AI systems at work and for myself, and i learn best by doing. so i make myself little interactive lessons, the kind you click through instead of read.

then one of them taught me something i didn't expect. i'd been writing rules for an AI agent in all caps, and she kept missing them. the lesson showed me the problem wasn't her. it was how i was teaching.

that felt too useful to keep to myself. i want others to learn with me.

so this is where those go. not everything i learn, just the lessons that changed something.

what makes it here

  • it changed what i did. not just what i know. i rebuilt something because of it.
  • it changed how i see me doing it. the lesson landed on me, not only on the tool.
  • you don't need my context. if it only makes sense inside my life, it stays in my notes.
  • you learn it by doing. every lesson is something you click through.
